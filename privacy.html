Privacy Policy — Time Imposter

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Time Imposter ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Time Imposter does not require account creation, login, or any personal information to play. The App does not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and other technical data as described in Google's Advertising Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's advertising settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android devices).

Data Storage

The App does not use accounts and does not transmit gameplay data to any server operated by us. All game state (player names, scores, round results) exists only temporarily on your device during play and is not saved or transmitted after the session ends.

Children's Privacy

Time Imposter is a party/drinking game intended for adult users. The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. See Google's policies linked above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
